Circular NFSC/CIR/2025/02

Issued 3 March 2025 · Effective 1 April 2025 · Status: In force

Amendment to the Cybersecurity and Operational Resilience Framework

1. Purpose

This circular amends Circular NFSC/CIR/2024/07. Provisions of the earlier circular not addressed here remain in force and unamended.

2. Amendment to incident reporting

Section 5 of Circular NFSC/CIR/2024/07 is amended. The notification window for a material cyber incident is reduced from seventy-two hours to twenty-four hours from detection. The threshold for financial loss constituting a material incident is reduced from NGN 10 million to NGN 5 million.

3. Amendment to the implementation schedule

The compliance date for encryption of customer data at rest for institutions with total assets below NGN 5 billion is brought forward from 31 December 2025 to 30 September 2025.

4. New requirement: third-party concentration reporting

Institutions shall submit a return listing all outsourced technology providers supporting a critical business service. The first return is due 30 June 2025 and thereafter annually.

5. Clarification

A shared Chief Information Security Officer arrangement may cover no more than three institutions, and the officer shall be resident in Nigeria.
